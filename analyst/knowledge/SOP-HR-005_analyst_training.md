# SOP-HR-005 Analyst Training and Qualification
Synthetic training document.

## 1. Onboarding
New analysts complete GMP, data integrity and good documentation practice (GDocP) training in their first 30 days and shadow a qualified analyst for at least 20 tests.

## 2. Qualification
An analyst is qualified on a method after three consecutive passing demonstration runs. Until then, a second person must verify each record.

## 3. Cohort monitoring
When several analysts join together, supervisors review their documentation quality weekly for the first 3 months. A rise in Documentation deviations for a cohort triggers refresher training within 2 weeks.

## 4. Records
Training records must be complete before an analyst signs any GMP record.
