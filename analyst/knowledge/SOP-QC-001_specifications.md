# SOP-QC-001 Release Specifications and Reporting of Results
Synthetic training document. Not a real company procedure.

## 1. Specification limits
| Method | Unit | Lower limit | Upper limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assay | % label claim | 95.0 | 105.0 |
| Related Substances (total) | % | none | 0.5 |
| Dissolution (Q) | % dissolved | 80.0 | 110.0 |
| Water Content (Karl Fischer) | % w/w | none | 2.0 |
| Residual Solvents | ppm | none | 5000 |

## 2. Reporting rules
A result is reported as recorded. A value outside its limits is an Out-of-Specification (OOS) result and must be reported to the supervisor the same day. Results must never be rounded, deleted or replaced to bring them within limits.

## 3. Status vs result
The status field (Pass or OOS) must agree with the recorded value and the limits above. A mismatch is a data-integrity finding and must be raised as a deviation of category Data Integrity.
