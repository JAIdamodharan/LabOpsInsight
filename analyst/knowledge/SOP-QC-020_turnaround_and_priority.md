# SOP-QC-020 Turnaround Time, Due Dates and Priority
Synthetic training document.

## 1. Due dates
Routine samples are due 5 calendar days after receipt. Urgent samples are due 3 calendar days after receipt. On-time delivery is the share of tests completed on or before the sample's due date.

## 2. Targets
The on-time delivery target is 92%. Below 85% for a month requires a documented recovery plan from the lab head.

## 3. Common causes of late delivery
Instrument breakdown with a shared backup, long waiting time before testing starts, repeated sample preparation, and unplanned OOS investigations.

## 4. Early warning
Samples that have waited more than 3 days before testing starts are flagged to the supervisor, because waiting time is the strongest single predictor of a late report.
