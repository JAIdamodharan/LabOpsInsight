# SOP-EQ-011 Instrument Downtime, Backup Instruments and Scheduling
Synthetic training document.

## 1. Breakdowns
A broken instrument is tagged out of use and logged as a Breakdown event with start and end dates. No test may start on a tagged instrument. A test recorded on an instrument during a breakdown is a data-review finding.

## 2. Backup instruments
Each instrument type has a named backup. LC-MS work normally runs on LCMS-01 or LCMS-02; when one is down the other takes the full queue.

## 3. Scheduling impact
When only one LC-MS is available the Related Substances queue grows. Supervisors must re-prioritise urgent samples, consider borrowing capacity from another lab, and warn the client when the delay will exceed the due date.

## 4. Preventive maintenance
Preventive maintenance is scheduled in low-load weeks and announced two weeks ahead.
