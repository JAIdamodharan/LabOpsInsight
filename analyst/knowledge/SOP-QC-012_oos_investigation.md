# SOP-QC-012 Investigation of Out-of-Specification Results
Synthetic training document.

## 1. Purpose
Defines how an OOS result is investigated so that the cause is found before any retest is considered.

## 2. Phase I: laboratory investigation (within 2 working days)
The analyst and supervisor check, in this order: (a) calculation and transcription errors; (b) system suitability results for the run; (c) instrument status, including the date of the last calibration and any breakdown or drift; (d) reference standard and reagent lots; (e) sample preparation and analyst technique.
If an instrument is found out of calibration or drifting, all results produced since the last good calibration check are in scope, not only the failing one.

## 3. Phase II: full investigation
If Phase I does not find an assignable laboratory cause, QA leads a full investigation including manufacturing review. Retesting is allowed only under an approved plan and never to average away a failing result.

## 4. Clusters of OOS results
Three or more OOS results on the same instrument and method within one month are treated as a cluster. A cluster triggers an instrument check and a Major deviation even when each single result has an explanation.
