# SOP-QA-003 Deviation Management and CAPA
Synthetic training document.

## 1. Categories and severity
Categories: Documentation, Equipment, Process, Sample Handling, Data Integrity. Severity: Minor, Major, Critical. Data Integrity deviations are never Minor.

## 2. Timelines
Deviations are opened within 1 working day of discovery. Minor: close within 15 days. Major: 30 days. Critical: 45 days, with QA head notification within 24 hours.

## 3. CAPA
A Corrective and Preventive Action is required for every Major and Critical deviation and for any category that repeats three times in a quarter.

## 4. Trend review
QA reviews deviation counts monthly by category, team and owner. A step increase in one category concentrated in one team indicates a training or process gap, not random error.
